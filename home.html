How it works

The mastery loop, in four steps.

1

Placement test

A 15-minute adaptive test places your student at their exact skill level — not their grade level.

2

Daily packet

Three short worksheets every day, 20 problems each, targeted at one specific skill.

3

95% mastery

Hit 95% accuracy for 5 days in a row, and the system automatically advances your student to the next level.

4

Track everything

Streaks, accuracy, attendance, level advances. All visible to parents and (for schools) teachers in real time.
